# What Is Context Engineering

Context Engineering is the practice of structuring what an AI sees, knows, and remembers — so it works *with* your codebase instead of against your token budget.

## Core Principle

**Give the AI the minimum context to produce the maximum quality output.**

Not everything. Not nothing. The right things, at the right time, in the right structure.

## What It Solves

| Problem | How Context Engineering Fixes It |
|---------|--------------------------------|
| Token Drain | Focused context = fewer wasted reads, less aimless reasoning, shorter responses |
| Drift | Explicit conventions = AI follows your patterns, not generic best practices |
| Session amnesia | Persistent memory = learnings carry across conversations |
| Full-codebase scans | Navigation layers = AI knows where to look before it reads |

## The Mindset Shift

- **Old**: "How do I write a better prompt?"
- **New**: "How do I build an environment where a simple prompt is enough?"

Context Engineering isn't about working harder per task. It's about building infrastructure that makes every task cheaper and more accurate.
